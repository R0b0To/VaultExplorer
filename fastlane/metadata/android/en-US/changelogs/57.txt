### UI & Theming
* **Interactive Floating Theme Editor:** Redesigned the theme editor into a floating picker accessible anywhere in the app. Tap UI elements directly to modify colors with instant live preview.
* **Redesigned Image Editor UI:** A cleaner, more intuitive interface for editing images.
* **General UI/UX Polish:** Fixed various layout glitches and visual inconsistencies across the app.

### File Management & Dual Pane
* **Drag-and-Drop Support:** Long-press and drag selected items or files directly into different folders and tabs.
* **Independent Dual-Pane Tabs:** In landscape mode, each pane now manages its own independent tab group without mirroring or interfering with the other.
* **Drawer Vault Behavior:** Vaults unlocked directly from the side drawer now open automatically.

### Camera & Capture
* **Non-blocking Video Saving:** Saving captured videos no longer freezes or locks the camera page. You can return immediately to the file manager while the save finishes as a background file transfer.
* **Segmented Recording & Quick Snaps:** Added support for recording in segments and fast photo/video captures that bypass the preview screen (fully compatible with automation workflows).
* **Pause & In-Recording Snapshots:** You can now pause active video recordings and take still snapshots while recording.
* **Background Recording:** Quick camera now continues recording seamlessly when minimized or when the screen turns off.

### Media Player
* **Picture-in-Picture (PiP) & Background Audio:** Added support for PiP and continuous background playback after leaving the media player *(disabled by default, can be enabled in settings)*.
* **Lock Controls Mode:** Added a lock button to prevent accidental taps, swipes, and pinch gestures. Press and hold to unlock; the system back button is also blocked while locked.
* **Gesture Swipe-to-Dismiss:** Quickly dismiss items in playlists by swiping perpendicularly to the playlist scroll direction (e.g., swipe down on horizontal playlists, swipe sideways on vertical ones).

### Security
* **Biometric Invalidation:** Master lock and vault biometric authentication now automatically disable and revert to password unlock (clearing cached vault credentials) whenever a new fingerprint or facial profile is enrolled on the device.

### Bug Fixes
* **AVIF Images:** Fixed a crash when attempting to edit AVIF format images.
* **Audio Waveform Visualizer:** Fixed an issue where the waveform visualizer in the audio editor failed to render long audio tracks.
* **7-Zip Archives:** Resolved edge-case errors preventing certain `.7z` archives from opening or extracting correctly.
