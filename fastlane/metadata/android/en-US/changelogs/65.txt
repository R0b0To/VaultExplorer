### Gallery
- Local-storage gallery scans now save and reuse metadata indexes when thumbnail caching uses app storage.
- Improved the gallery folder-scope choices for readability.
- Added section markers to fast scrolling through grouped gallery results.

### Media viewer
- Added Motion Photo playback with a saved autoplay preference across viewer opens and playlist navigation.
- Added interactive 360° panorama viewing for supported images; playlist swipes are disabled while viewing.
- HEIC and HEIF images now show detailed metadata, and portrait HEIC images keep their correct fit when viewer controls open.

### File browsing
- Improved folder refresh handling when folders are exposed through document providers.
