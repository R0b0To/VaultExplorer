### Vault settings
- Added per-vault and Local Storage options for thumbnail caches, gallery metadata indexes, and music indexes, with actions to clear saved data.
- Grouped per-vault controls in a new **Indexes & thumbnails** page.

### Container tools
- Combined VeraCrypt, LUKS, and BitLocker expansion in one format-aware tool, with size previews, free-space checks, and quick size controls.
- Translated the volume expansion interface across supported languages.

### File manager & sync
- Improved Vault Sync comparisons with clearer source and target summaries, difference filters, per-item actions, and queued change counts.
- Reduced repeated storage-provider checks during transfers and improved scroll-position retention when files or layouts change.

### Reliability
- Improved Android document-provider access checks and diagnostics.
- Added native HMAC and AES-CBC primitives with platform fallback.
- Removed redundant password hints and clarified the PIM default.
