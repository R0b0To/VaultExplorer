Vault Explorer opens and browses encrypted disk containers directly on
your device -- VeraCrypt/TrueCrypt volumes, LUKS, BitLocker, and raw
VHD/VHDX images -- without exporting your files unencrypted to shared
storage.

Features:
* Read/write NTFS, FAT, and ext2/3/4 filesystems inside a container
* VeraCrypt/LUKS/BitLocker unlock, including cascaded ciphers
* Built-in media preview for photos and video stored inside a vault
* Create new encrypted containers and USB volumes
* Biometric unlock

Vault Explorer does not collect or transmit any data. All decryption
happens locally on your device.
